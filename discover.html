Our Story!

Spark of Innovation

I am Monishka, a BCA student. It all began when I heard about the accelerator program which was going to be conducted in the university. Basically this accelerator program was structured on the fundamentals of business and startup principles. So I decided to participate in the program which was a big catalyst. This program lasted for three months. I dedicated these three months in intense learning, pouring my heart and soul into every effort. Meanwhile the program an idea struck me which resulted in the innovation of my own startup, BLISSORA.

Dedication for Development

While I was thinking about Blissora, I was intrigued by the concept of convertible heels into flats. I delved into extensive research, meticulously exploring the potential ramifications and crafting a compelling presentation that showcased the myriad possibilities these innovative footwear could unlock.

Funding

After meticulously crafting the Blissora presentation, I confidently pitched the innovative concept to the panel of esteemed mentors from the accelerator program and JECRC incubation. My compelling vision resonated and secured selection for the program. Through a series of captivating pitches, I ultimately received the prestigious honor of being awarded with a one lakh fund to propel Blissora's growth.

Unlocking the Mechanism

Armed with the newfound funding, I embarked on a quest to explore the intricacies of convertible heel mechanisms. My research delved into various existing solutions, including door stoppers, bulb holders, and other locking mechanisms. This meticulous investigation led me to discover a remarkably elegant and streamlined mechanism for seamlessly transforming heels into flats.

Finalized the Prototype

The moment I discovered the perfect locking mechanism to seamlessly transform heels into flats, I dove headfirst into the manufacturing process. I studied the intricacies of each component, exploring different materials and construction techniques. This in-depth understanding fueled up my creativity, culminating in the creation of Blissora's signature convertible heel mechanism. I finally brought my vision to life, crafting a unique and functional product that embodied my passion and dedication in the heels.

Another Big Grant

I recently received a grant of six lakh rupees which was more likely a dream come true. This substantial funding will be a game-changer, providing the much-needed resources to ramp up production and launch some of the marketing campaigns and will be looking at such opportunities. We are now one step closer to realizing vision and sharing our innovative convertible heels with the world.

Testing and Expansion

After the grant, I am more likely moving this startup into an established business. I am focusing on building the blissora strong and sustainable brand. Starting with the testing process, where I will be gathering valuable feedback before launching the product. By having strangers try out our convertible heels, through which I will gain authentic insights into their comfort and experience. This testing phase is crucial to ensure that my design is not only innovative but also meets the needs of the target audience.
